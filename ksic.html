ABOUT KSIC

What is KSIC?

Official English name · to be announced

KSIC is a university network built at Kazakhstani universities to promote international exchange and global skills development among students.

Dream Bridge works with each university's KSIC chapter to build exchanges with Korean universities and institutions, international forums, education programs and youth networks.
